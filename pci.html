A SPARC platform that builds its own firmware: a PCIe-capable niagara machine in QEMU

No SPARC hardware was available during the development of the kit, so it is built and tested with TOOLCHAIN=sun on the QEMU-emulated Niagara machine itself, alongside TOOLCHAIN=gnu on Linux.

The OpenBoot image from the T2 archive (niagara2/release/openboot.bin) already contained the vpci dropin, which made it possible to bring up PCI on this T1 platform initially, more than a month before OpenBoot could be rebuilt from scratch.

A native Solaris build needs I/O, hence QEMU patches for devices under the root complex. The patches and the kit grew together and each is needed to test the other: the kit's pci configuration (TARGET=pci) needs the patched QEMU, and the patches need firmware from the kit's sources.

In the Solaris 10 guest, TOOLCHAIN=sun builds TARGET=legion (the default, a machine without PCI) with each of eight Sun compiler environments, cc 5.8 to 5.15 (see REQUIREMENTS.SOLARIS and build_matrix). Every one of these builds, booted on the Linux host, reaches a Solaris login, which shows that the guest works as a build environment (TOOLCHAIN=gnu, built on Linux with gcc 13 to 16, reaches the same login as well).

At the moment only the first patch of the series is under consideration (v2 thread on qemu-devel): the maintainer would queue it, but QEMU's current policy does not accept AI-generated code, and the policy is being updated, though not in time for the 11.2 release cycle.

The other patches the pci configuration needs are not published yet.
